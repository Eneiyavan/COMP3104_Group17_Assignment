George Brown College

George Brown College is a public college located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

The college offers career-focused programs in technology, business, hospitality, design, and health sciences.

It emphasizes practical education, hands-on learning, and industry-related experience.

Academic Programs

George Brown College offers programs that help students build technical, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Practical Learning

Hands-on projects help students apply classroom knowledge to realistic workplace situations.
