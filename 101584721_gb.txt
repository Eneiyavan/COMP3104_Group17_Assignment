# 1. History
George Brown College was established in 1967 and opened its doors to students in 1968. 
It was named after George Brown, a Canadian politician and one of the Fathers of Confederation.
 In 2025, the institution became known as George Brown Polytechnic, reflecting its focus on practical education and industry-related skills.

# 2. Campus Locations
George Brown Polytechnic is located in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
 It has three main campuses: Casa Loma Campus, St. James Campus, and Waterfront Campus. 
 Each campus offers different programs and learning facilities for students.

# 3. Programs and Courses
George Brown Polytechnic offers a variety of programs in areas such as computer technology, business, health sciences, design, hospitality, construction, and community services. 
Students can choose from certificates, diplomas, degrees, postgraduate programs, and apprenticeship training, depending on their career goals.

# 4. Student Life and Services
George Brown Polytechnic provides services to support students throughout their education. 
These include academic advising, career services, library resources, student clubs, and wellness support.
 Students can also participate in campus activities and access resources to help them develop their academic, professional, and personal skills.

 # 5. Diversity and Community
George Brown Polytechnic welcomes a large and diverse group of students, including domestic and international students from many backgrounds.
 Being in Toronto, one of the most multicultural cities in the world, adds to this mix and gives students the chance to learn alongside people from different cultures and experiences.
The college offers support for students who are new to Canada or to post-secondary education, such as orientation programs, language and learning supports, and accessibility services.
It also encourages inclusion and community involvement through student clubs, events and partnerships with local organizations, helping students feel connected both on campus and in the city.
